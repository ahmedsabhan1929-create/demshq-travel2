Our Philosophy

MORE THAN A MEAL.
AN OCEAN LEGACY.

Long before a dish reaches your rooftop table, its story has already begun in the azure depths of the Aegean and Marmara seas. At Adella Seafood, we craft an ode to Istanbul’s maritime heritage.

Situated in Historic Fatih on Hamidiye Caddesi, our spacious rooftop provides an unobstructed panorama of the Bosphorus Strait. Every plate combines centuries-old Ottoman seafood wisdom with contemporary Mediterranean refinement.
